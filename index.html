UAE e-invoicing

E-invoicing is becoming mandatory. Use the change to fix the whole back office.

Under the UAE's e-invoicing programme, invoices move to a structured digital format sent through an accredited service provider, and PDF invoices will no longer count. We get you ready to comply and use the same project to speed up quoting and collections.

Rollout dates depend on business size and are set by the Federal Tax Authority. We confirm the dates that apply to you during discovery.

FAQ

Common questions

Will AI send emails, quotes or payments without us checking?

No. Every pack has a review step. AI reads, extracts and drafts, and someone on your team approves anything that goes to a client, supplier or bank. Where the AI isn't confident, it flags the item for review and leaves it unsent.

Do you handle our money?

Never. We prepare payment runs, WPS salary files and bank files, and connect your systems to your bank. You or your bank release the payments through your own approval process. Where open finance access needs a licence, we work with licensed providers.

What systems do you work with?

Mainly Zoho (Books, CRM, Inventory, One) and Tally, plus other ERPs and CRMs. We connect them using automation tools such as n8n and leading AI models. If you don't have a proper system yet, setting one up is part of the work.

Is the AI accurate enough for invoices and prices?

Before go-live, we test every AI step against a set of your real, hand-checked documents and agree accuracy targets with you. The AI never makes up prices or part numbers. Totals are calculated by the system, not by the AI.

What about data privacy?

We design around the UAE Personal Data Protection Law, sign an NDA before discovery, give you a written note on how your data is handled, and keep access to the minimum each workflow needs.

How much does it cost?

Discovery is a fixed fee that is credited against the build. Each pack is quoted at a fixed price after discovery. Ongoing support is a monthly retainer. Software licences are billed to you directly with no markup.
